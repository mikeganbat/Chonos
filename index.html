About Chonos

A dependable partner on site.

CHONOS CONSTRUCTION works with builders and contractors to deliver dependable steel fixing and construction support. We value clear communication, safe work practices and quality workmanship on every project.

Our team is focused on helping projects run efficiently, meeting site requirements and delivering work to the agreed scope and schedule.

Our Services

Steel fixing and construction services for residential, commercial and civil works.

Reinforcement Steel Fixing

02 / CONCRETE STRUCTURES

Slabs & Footings
